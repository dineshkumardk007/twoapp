# Terms of Service & Relational Safety Disclaimers

*Last Updated: September 2026*

---

## 1. Age Restriction: 18+ Only

Two is built exclusively for consenting adults aged 18 and older. The application contains intimate relationship tools, optional adult conversation decks, and complex conflict scaffolding that are not designed or legally certified for minors.

---

## 2. Non-Therapy & Medical Disclaimer

**Two is NOT a licensed healthcare provider, psychotherapist, or medical device.**

* The **Conflict Repair Kit**, **Emotional Weather Report**, and **Ask-For-What-You-Need Menu** are communication scaffolding tools intended to facilitate grounded conversations between consenting adults.
* They do not constitute marriage counseling, psychotherapy, psychiatric diagnosis, or clinical intervention.
* The application will never assign a numerical "health score" to your relationship or diagnose mental health conditions.

### Immediate Crisis Resources
If you, your partner, or someone you know is experiencing acute emotional distress, domestic violence, or a mental health emergency, please immediately contact local emergency services or professional hotlines:
* **United States / Canada:** Call or text `988` (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or `1-800-799-SAFE` (National Domestic Violence Hotline).
* **United Kingdom:** Call `111` (NHS Mental Health) or `0808 2000 247` (National Domestic Abuse Helpline).
* **India:** Call `14416` (Tele-MANAS) or `181` (Women Helpline).
* **International:** Visit [Befrienders Worldwide](https://www.befrienders.org/) or [Find A Helpline](https://findahelpline.com/).

## 3. Strict Non-Surveillance Commitment (Anti-Stalkerware Guarantee)

Couples software is too often misused as a vector for covert control, digital stalking, and intimate partner surveillance. Two explicitly rejects these mechanics:

1. **No Background Location Tracking:** Two does not offer 24/7 background location sharing. Location pings are strictly time-boxed (15m, 1h, 4h) and visible on screen with an active countdown banner.
2. **No Secret Alerts:** If either partner leaves the space or wipes their device, the app will never sound an alarm, send a notification, or report exit events to the remaining partner.
3. **No Screenshot Alarms or Forced Read Receipts:** The app enforces read receipts as completely optional, off by default, and asymmetrical (you choose what you share, never forced by your partner).
4. **Exit-Safe Quick Wipe:** Either partner has the fundamental right to tap **Emergency Quick Exit** to instantly shred all local cryptographic keys and local database records.
